1. Purpose of This Prototype

Fidelo E-Bike Store is a working prototype of a centralized, role-based inventory management and customer application system designed for a multi-location e-bike business. It replaces scattered spreadsheets, manual stock counts, and disconnected communication with a connected workflow covering product cataloging, location-specific inventory, inter-branch stock transfers, customer product applications, low-stock notifications, movement audit trails, reports, and user administration.

The prototype allows the client and project stakeholders to review the design, navigation, workflows, and core functions before the system is considered ready for final production use.

Prototype status: The client may explore and test the system now, but only test or demonstration data should be entered. Real customer records, financial information, and live business operations should not yet be processed through this staging version.

2. System Overview

The system is organized around distinct user roles, each with tailored interfaces and permissions.

User RolePrimary Responsibilities
Visitor Browse the landing page and public product catalogue, view product details and specifications, access contact information, and navigate to privacy policy, terms of service, login, and registration pages.
Customer Browse and search the product catalogue, view detailed product information, submit product applications, track application status, manage profile and avatar, and receive application-related notifications.
Branch Staff Manage branch-specific inventory and stock levels, initiate and receive inter-branch stock transfers, review and process customer product applications, set low-stock thresholds, generate and export operational reports, and manage profile.
Warehouse Staff Manage central warehouse inventory, perform stock-in and stock-out operations, dispatch stock to branch locations via transfers, monitor warehouse movements, and generate inventory reports.
Administrator Full system access including user management, staff account creation, product and category CRUD, location management, authentication audit logs, system-wide reports, and all inventory and transfer operations.

3. Main Prototype Workflow

The following sequence illustrates the complete lifecycle of inventory management and customer interactions within the system:

  1. Product Discovery: A visitor browses the landing page and public product catalogue, views e-bike details with images and specifications, then registers or signs in.
  2. Customer Application: The customer selects a product, submits an application with their details, and receives a confirmation. The application enters a "Pending" state.
  3. Application Review: Branch staff or administrators review the incoming application, inspect customer details, and move it to "Under Review" status.
  4. Application Decision: Staff approve the application (advancing it toward completion) or reject it with a reason. The customer receives a notification of the status change.
  5. Inventory Adjustment: Upon application completion, inventory levels are adjusted. Staff perform stock-out operations to reflect fulfilled applications.
  6. Stock Replenishment: When branch inventory runs low, staff create an inter-branch transfer request from the warehouse or another branch. The source location approves and dispatches the stock, and the receiving branch confirms receipt.
  7. Audit and Reporting: Every inventory change generates an immutable stock movement record with before/after quantities. Administrators and staff generate reports and export CSV files for operational analysis.
  8. Notifications: The system delivers targeted in-app notifications for low-stock alerts, application status updates, and transfer progress to relevant users.

9. Prototype Features Available for Evaluation

The prototype includes the following technical implementations and interface features:

  • Responsive public website with product catalogue for desktop and mobile screens
  • Email/password authentication with registration, email verification, forgot-password reset flow, and role-based dashboard routing
  • Product catalogue with images stored in MinIO, specifications, pricing, category filtering, and keyword search
  • Product category management with CRUD operations and active/inactive toggling
  • Customer product application system with submission, multi-step review pipeline, and status tracking
  • Location-specific inventory management with individual stock levels and configurable low-stock thresholds
  • Stock-in and stock-out operations with append-only movement history recording before/after quantities
  • Inter-branch transfer workflow covering request creation, approval/rejection, dispatch, and receipt with full audit trail
  • In-app notification system delivering low-stock alerts, application status updates, and transfer progress to targeted users
  • Operational reports with data grids and CSV export for inventory, movements, transactions, transfers, and applications
  • User administration with staff account provisioning, role assignment, location scoping, and account activation controls
  • Authentication audit logging capturing login attempts, password changes, and security events with timestamps
  • Profile management with avatar uploads stored in MinIO object storage across all roles
  • CSRF protection, rate-limiting on authentication endpoints, and secure HTTP-only cookie-based JWT sessions
  • Multi-location architecture supporting distinct branch and warehouse inventories under a unified system

10. Suggested Client Evaluation

The client should evaluate the prototype using the following end-to-end scenario:

  1. Browse the landing page, public product catalogue, and legal pages as a visitor.
  2. Register a test customer account and verify the email.
  3. Sign in as the customer, browse the product catalogue, view a product's details, and submit an application.
  4. Sign in as a branch staff member, review the incoming application, and advance it through the review pipeline.
  5. Perform a stock-out operation on the branch inventory to reflect the fulfilled application, then complete it.
  6. Observe the low-stock notification when inventory falls below the configured threshold.
  7. Create an inter-branch transfer request from the warehouse to replenish branch stock.
  8. Sign in as warehouse staff, approve and dispatch the transfer.
  9. Return as branch staff to receive the incoming transfer and confirm updated stock levels.
  10. Navigate to reports, filter by date range, and export a CSV report.
  11. Sign in as an administrator, review users, audit logs, products, categories, and locations.
  12. Return as the customer to view the updated application status and check notifications.
  13. Check the system on both desktop and mobile devices.
